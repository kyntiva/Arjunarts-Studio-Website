About The Studio

A dedicated sanctuary for movement and artistic growth.

"Art is not just technique; it is the rhythm of human expression brought to life with grace and soul."

Founded by Vrindha and rooted in the cultural heart of Panampilly Nagar, Kochi, Arjunarts Studio exists to nurture artistic expression across diverse forms of movement.

Whether honoring timeless traditions through Indian classical dance or exploring dynamic rhythms through Western forms, our studio welcomes learners, performers, and creative seekers of all backgrounds.

From studio training to specialized workshops and stage celebrations, we believe in cultivating an environment that is artistically rigorous, aesthetically warm, and deeply human.

The Vision

Meet Vrindha

Founder & Artistic Director of Arjunarts Studio.

With a sincere commitment to the performing arts, Vrindha established Arjunarts Studio in Panampilly Nagar to serve as an authentic creative haven. Her approach blends respect for foundational discipline with the pure joy of spontaneous creative expression.

Under her guidance, the studio remains dedicated to mentoring dancers, facilitating artistic collaboration, and bringing thoughtful performing arts to life across Kochi.
